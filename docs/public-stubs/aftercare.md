# Aftercare — public stub _muse

**_muse note:** Rewritten public stub. Check-in structure only; no
personal ritual content.

After a session, each participant is offered a short, neutral check-in:

- How was that, in your own words?
- Is there anything you want to flag for the other person or for a host?
- Do you want to close here, or is there a next step you both agree to?

Aftercare is optional to complete in detail, and closing is always
available. Nothing in aftercare re-opens consent for a further session;
a new session needs a new, mutual confirmation.
