# Pair agreement — public stub _muse

**_muse note:** Rewritten public stub. Short, neutral pair-agreement
form only; personal bond-map content is NOT copied here.

Two people who plan to meet regularly may write a short pair agreement:

- Boundaries, in the touch-language terms above.
- Check-ins: how often, and in what form.
- Exit: either person can end the agreement, or a single session,
  at any time, without a reason.
- Review: the agreement is revisited when either person asks, and it
  does not carry over automatically to a new kind of contact.

A pair agreement is not a contract for touch, and it does not replace
in-the-moment consent.
