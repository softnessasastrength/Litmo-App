# Touch language — public stub _muse

**_muse note:** Rewritten public stub, not a copy of personal wording.
Axes and map only; no personal examples, no personal scripts.

Touch is a language, not a transaction. In this app, a person states, for
themselves, in advance and in the moment:

- **Welcomed:** contact types and zones they welcome, in general terms.
- **Ask first:** contact that always needs a fresh, specific ask, every time.
- **Soft limit:** contact that needs extra care and a slower check-in, and
  can move to ask-first or off-limits at any time.
- **Off limits:** contact that is not on the table.

Rules that do not change:

- The strictest boundary wins.
- A welcomed item alone is never session consent.
- A match, a profile, or a history is never consent.
- Preferences that are missing, stale, or contradictory fail closed:
  exclude, do not invent a welcome.
- Every session still needs a mutual, explicit, in-the-moment confirmation.
