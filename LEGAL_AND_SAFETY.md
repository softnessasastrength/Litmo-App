# LEGAL_AND_SAFETY — _muse

**_muse note:** Prepared by Muse (_muse) from the separation proposal, for
Branden's review. Every item below is **OPEN**, not done, not solved, not
guaranteed. This file does not state that any item has been implemented,
reviewed, priced, or approved.

1. **Identity verification, implemented — OPEN.** Current original-repo
   state, per its own liability doc: not implemented; discovery/matches/
   profiles in the current build are synthetic. Needs a decided verification
   tier, a user-facing statement that identity is not safety, and a plan
   for failures, duplicates, and re-verification after a ban.
2. **Incident reporting and response pipeline, operated — OPEN.** Design
   and code exist; an operated pipeline does not. Needs in-app reporting
   reachable mid- and post-session, a response SLA, interim suspension
   while review is pending, a graduated response framework, a
   law-enforcement-contact policy that does not pressure the reporter,
   and a retention policy for sensitive data.
3. **A human safety / trust team, or a decided alternative — OPEN.**
   Per the original liability doc's own lean: this cannot be deferred
   until after the first report. No staffing, rota, or second-reviewer
   arrangement is in this repo.
4. **Attorney-reviewed terms of use and liability boundary — OPEN.**
   Hard dependency, not a nice-to-have: terms stating the app coordinates
   consent and communication, does not vet, endorse, or guarantee any
   user's safety, and is not a substitute for personal judgment or
   professional care. Jurisdiction-specific terms are open.
5. **Insurance, researched and priced — OPEN.** Per the original
   liability doc: not researched. General/platform liability for
   user-to-user harm, E&O for verification/moderation systems, and
   whether standard tech E&O excludes physical harm between users are
   all open. This may be the highest-cost item.
6. **App-store and payment-processor review — OPEN.** Per the original
   liability doc: not researched. "Non-sexual, platonic touch between
   strangers, coordinated by an app" does not map cleanly onto existing
   store or processor categories, even with an explicitly non-dating,
   non-sexual framing.
7. **A business model that pays for items 1–6 — OPEN.** Blocked in the
   original repo by the non-monetization covenant, which has NOT been
   copied here and has NOT been dropped silently. Licence and
   monetization stance are undecided and must be decided explicitly.
8. **Location and meeting safety policy — OPEN.** No decided policy in
   this repo on where sessions happen, whether first sessions are hosted
   or public-place-only, or what a host's role is.
9. **A vetted-community launch plan, not an open marketplace — OPEN.**
   No plan in this repo for inviting, vetting, hosting, and supporting
   a first small group. Beta walkthroughs in the original repo are not
   that plan.
10. **Clinical / therapeutic boundary, in user-facing language — OPEN.**
    Internal principle carried over: this app does not diagnose, treat,
    certify safety, or replace professional care. That boundary must
    appear in onboarding, in any store listing, and in marketing — not
    only in an internal doc — and must hold when users ask the app to
    do more.
